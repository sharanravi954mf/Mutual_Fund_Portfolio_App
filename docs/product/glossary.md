# Product Glossary

## Purpose
This document defines the key financial, technical, and operational terms utilized across the Sharan Fincorp platform.

## Audience
New developers, testers, advisors, and clients.

## Business Context
Consistent terminology prevents misunderstandings during feature reviews, code comments, and stakeholder updates.

---

## Detailed Explanation

- **Asset Management Company (AMC)**: A financial firm that pools investor capital to purchase securities (commonly known as a mutual fund house).
- **Folio**: A unique account number allocated by an AMC/Registrar to track investments made by an investor within that fund house.
- **Scheme**: A specific mutual fund investment product issued by an AMC (e.g. equity fund, debt fund).
- **Net Asset Value (NAV)**: The per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme, calculated at the end of each business day.
- **Extended Internal Rate of Return (XIRR)**: The annualized rate of return for a series of cash flows occurring at irregular intervals (the industry standard for mutual fund returns).
- **Assets Under Management (AUM)**: The total market value of all investments managed by an Advisor or distributor.
- **Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA)**: Operational agencies (like CAMS or KFintech) that maintain investor records, process transactions, and issue statements on behalf of AMCs.
- **CAMS (Computer Age Management Services)**: India's largest RTA, managing the majority of mutual fund assets.
- **KFintech (KFin Technologies)**: A major RTA managing mutual fund records.
- **Permanent Account Number (PAN)**: A unique 10-character alphanumeric identifier issued by the Indian Income Tax Department, used as primary identity verification for investments.
- **RTA Mailback**: Automated email reports sent by RTAs to distributors containing transaction database backups (usually packaged in zip files containing DBF statements).
- **Verification**: The step-by-step security process that authorizes a client login account to link to an existing investor profile and folio.
- **Advisor**: The financial distributor using the Admin Console to manage clients, ingestion, and invoice overlays.
- **Investor**: The individual investor viewing their assets on the Client Console.
- **Nominee**: An individual appointed by the investor to receive assets in the event of the investor's death.
